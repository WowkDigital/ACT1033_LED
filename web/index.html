ACT1033 LED panel

Compose something for your 64×16 panel and send it to your own Cloudflare Worker. Your ESP32 picks it up and shows it. Everything is rendered in this browser; the token never leaves it except to talk to your Worker.

The Worker refreshes the next departures every minute, so the panel keeps running on its own. Sending any other picture to this panel switches this off.

Best: 64×16 pixels, one shared palette of at most 16 colours, up to 64 KB. The file is sent unchanged.
